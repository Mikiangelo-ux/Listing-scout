Three steps from idea to answer.

  1. Enter a keyword

    The tool sends a keyword search for active public listings to the Etsy Open API.

  2. Read the signals

    It reads titles, tags, prices and favorites, and compares them across the results.

  3. Get a short report

    A brief summary helps the owner decide whether the idea is worth pursuing.

Small on purpose.

What it does

  • Searches public, active listings by keyword
  • Reads titles, tags, prices and favorites
  • Keeps requests few and rate-limited

What it doesn’t do

  • Create or edit listings
  • Read sales data or send emails
  • Scrape pages, or share or resell data
  • Charge anyone, or have other users
